About the Lab

Crafted with uncompromising precision

Ceramica Dental Lab is a Dubai-based dental laboratory working with 350+ clinics in over 50 countries worldwide. The lab focuses on high-end aesthetic restorations and international collaboration with dentists seeking exceptional results.

After years of research and development, Ceramica introduced its signature Micro-Layering Technique, where ultra-thin ceramic layers are built under the microscope. This method enhances translucency, light reflection, and natural texture, creating restorations that closely mimic natural teeth.

Our Signature Specialty

The Art of
Micro-Layering

Micro-Layering is a ceramic build-up technique where ultra-fine layers are applied under a microscope, reaching up to 12 individual layers within a very minimal thickness.

This allows the highest possible number of layers while maintaining the thinnest ceramic structure, creating natural depth, translucency, and optical effects similar to natural teeth.

The technology is a patented technique, certified by the Dubai Chamber of Commerce and Economy and protected under the Madrid Protocol for the international protection of intellectual and commercial rights.
